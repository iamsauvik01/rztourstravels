Overview

Meghalaya, made for two

Meghalaya suits two people very well. Clouds drift past the window at breakfast, roads empty into silence, and every viewpoint seems to have been made for a pair of chairs. Honeymoon in the Clouds is our most indulgent itinerary, built around beautiful places to stay and the time to enjoy them.

You’ll spend your first evening watching the sun go down over Umiam Lake from a lakeside retreat, explore Shillong at your own pace and share a private candlelight dinner. Then it’s up to Sohra and a boutique stay on the edge of the cliffs, with the Seven Sisters Falls and Nohkalikai just beyond the mist, before a day trip to the clear waters of Dawki.

A private cab and driver are with you throughout, and our team handles the small touches — flowers and cake on arrival, a photographer for a few hours if you’d like one, and flexible timings so nothing ever feels like a schedule.
