R&Z Tours and Travels is a small Shillong team that plans private journeys across Meghalaya — the way we’d show the hills to our own family.

We know which viewpoints clear by 9 am, which root-bridge trails turn slippery after rain, and which homestay serves the best jadoh. Every trip comes with a private vehicle, a driver who lives here, handpicked stays and a real person on WhatsApp whenever you need one.

Why travel with R&Z

The difference a local makes

Meghalaya rewards people who know it well. Weather shifts by the hour, the best places are often unsigned, and timing is everything.

Local experts

Born and raised in the Khasi Hills, our planners and drivers read the clouds, know the shortcuts and introduce you to the people behind the places.

Handpicked stays

Every hotel, resort and homestay is personally visited — chosen for warmth, cleanliness and a view worth waking up to.

Private vehicles

Your own clean, comfortable car and an experienced hill driver for the whole trip — no shared coaches, no fixed group timetables.

24/7 support

A real person on call and on WhatsApp throughout your journey, ready to re-plan around rain, roadworks or a change of heart.
