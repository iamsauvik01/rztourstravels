Frequently asked questions

October to May brings clear skies, cool days and the clearest water at Dawki. June to September is monsoon season — the waterfalls are spectacular and the hills are cloaked in mist, but some roads and treks can be affected by rain. We plan around whichever season you choose.

Most travellers fly or take the train into Guwahati (Assam). Shillong is about a three-hour drive from Guwahati airport, and all our packages include pick-up and drop there. Shillong Airport at Umroi has limited flights, which we can also cover.

It involves roughly 3,500 stone steps down from Tyrna to Nongriat — and the same back up. Anyone with reasonable fitness can do it at a steady pace, but it is tiring on the knees. We recommend our overnight version so you aren’t rushing, and porters can be arranged.

Absolutely. Every itinerary is private, so we can add or remove days, swap hotels for homestays, slow the pace for grandparents or add photography stops. Tell us what you have in mind in the form above.

Once you’re happy with your itinerary we’ll send a written quote. A part-advance confirms your hotels and vehicle, and the balance is due before travel. Exact terms and our cancellation policy are shared with your quote.

Indian citizens do not need an Inner Line Permit for Meghalaya. International travellers should carry their passport and visa at all times; we’ll brief you on any local registration rules in force at the time of your trip.
